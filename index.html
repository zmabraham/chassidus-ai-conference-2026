Proceedings

Summary of the Day

Three threads ran through nearly every session — then sixteen talks, expandable below.

01

Feed AI an authoritative, “kosher” corpus (RAG) rather than trusting its training data.

02

Build skills, agents, and instructions — then review the output. The last 2% matters.

03

AI is best for accessing, analyzing, translating, and synthesizing Torah, not for deciding or replacing your own learning.
